Resources

Primary Text Repositories and Digital Concordances

Digital libraries and concordance databases that provide searchable full-text corpora, sentence-aligned translations, and critical apparatuses for classical and medieval Chinese philosophical works.

  • Chinese Text Project (Donald Sturgeon / ctext.org) - The definitive open-access digital library of pre-modern Chinese thought, featuring full-text search, character concordances, and sentence-aligned English translations.
  • CBETA Online (Chinese Buddhist Electronic Text Association / DILA) - The standard scholarly digital edition of the Chinese Buddhist canon, integrating the Taishō Tripiṭaka and Zokuzōkyō with full-text search, punctuation, and dictionary lookup.
  • SAT Daizokyo Text Database (University of Tokyo) - A searchable textual database of the 85-volume Taishō Shinshū Daizōkyō complete with critical apparatus, linked directly to digital facsimile images and external Buddhist lexicons.
  • Scripta Sinica (Institute of History and Philology, Academia Sinica) - A massive digital collection of Chinese classical literature, dynastic histories, and philosophical masters with advanced textual search and variant-character matching.

Lexicons, Character Dictionaries, and Conceptual Thesauri

Specialized dictionaries and conceptual databases designed to analyze classical Chinese philosophical terminology, syntactic patterns, and historical character evolutions.

  • Thesaurus Linguae Sericae (Christoph Harbsmeier et al. / Heidelberg University & Kyoto University) - An analytical dictionary and conceptual encyclopedia of classical Chinese, categorizing synonym hierarchies, syntactic functions, and rhetorical structures across early texts.
  • Digital Dictionary of Buddhism & CJKV-E Dictionary (Charles Muller / University of Tokyo) - An authoritative, peer-reviewed lexicon of East Asian Buddhist technical terms, persons, and texts, accessible via free guest login.
  • ZDic (Handian) - A comprehensive digital Chinese dictionary integrating classical definitions from the Shuowen Jiezi, Kangxi Zidian, and modern character etymologies.
  • Chinese Etymology (Richard Sears) - A searchable paleographical archive displaying the historical evolution of individual characters from oracle bone and bronze script to seal script and modern standard forms.

Biographical, Historical, and Prosopographical Databases

Databases that document historical context, bureaucratic careers, kinship structures, and intellectual lineages of Chinese thinkers.

  • China Biographical Database Project (Harvard University, Academia Sinica & Peking University) - A freely accessible relational database tracking biographical data, teacher-student relationships, and social networks for over 500,000 historical Chinese figures.
  • China Historical GIS (Harvard University & Fudan University) - A digital historical atlas providing spatial and temporal datasets to locate historical settlements, administrative units, and regional academies across imperial dynasties.

Academic Societies, Scholarly Portals, and Bibliographic Registries

Scholarly clearinghouses, academic associations, and subject-specific bibliographies that track research developments, conferences, and literature in Chinese and comparative philosophy.

  • Warp, Weft, and Way (Stephen Angle & Manyul Im, eds.) - An active group blog and academic clearinghouse devoted to Chinese and comparative philosophy, hosting substantive debates, book announcements, and podcast episodes.
  • PhilPapers: Chinese Philosophy (JeeLoo Liu, ed.) - A comprehensive bibliographic database indexing tens of thousands of contemporary academic books, journal articles, and preprints across all subfields of Chinese thought.
  • International Society for Chinese Philosophy (ISCP) - The premier international academic society dedicated to Chinese philosophy, hosting biennial conferences and supporting cross-cultural philosophical exchange.
  • Society for Asian and Comparative Philosophy (SACP) - A scholarly organization promoting comparative work between Asian and Western traditions, hosting annual panels and monograph series.
  • The Golden Elixir: Taoism and Taoist Alchemy (Fabrizio Pregadio) - A specialized academic reference portal providing bibliographies, glossaries, textual introductions, and canonical indices for Daoist philosophy and internal alchemy (neidan).
