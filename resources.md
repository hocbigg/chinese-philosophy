# Resources

## Primary Text Repositories and Digital Concordances

Digital libraries and concordance databases that provide searchable full-text corpora, sentence-aligned translations, and critical apparatuses for classical and medieval Chinese philosophical works.

- [Chinese Text Project (Donald Sturgeon / ctext.org)](https://ctext.org/) - The definitive open-access digital library of pre-modern Chinese thought, featuring full-text search, character concordances, and sentence-aligned English translations.
- [CBETA Online (Chinese Buddhist Electronic Text Association / DILA)](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/) - The standard scholarly digital edition of the Chinese Buddhist canon, integrating the Taishō Tripiṭaka and Zokuzōkyō with full-text search, punctuation, and dictionary lookup.
- [SAT Daizokyo Text Database (University of Tokyo)](https://21dzk.l.u-tokyo.ac.jp/SAT2018/) - A searchable textual database of the 85-volume Taishō Shinshū Daizōkyō complete with critical apparatus, linked directly to digital facsimile images and external Buddhist lexicons.
- [Scripta Sinica (Institute of History and Philology, Academia Sinica)](https://hanchi.ihp.sinica.edu.tw/) - A massive digital collection of Chinese classical literature, dynastic histories, and philosophical masters with advanced textual search and variant-character matching.

## Lexicons, Character Dictionaries, and Conceptual Thesauri

Specialized dictionaries and conceptual databases designed to analyze classical Chinese philosophical terminology, syntactic patterns, and historical character evolutions.

- [Thesaurus Linguae Sericae (Christoph Harbsmeier et al. / Heidelberg University & Kyoto University)](https://hxwd.org/) - An analytical dictionary and conceptual encyclopedia of classical Chinese, categorizing synonym hierarchies, syntactic functions, and rhetorical structures across early texts.
- [Digital Dictionary of Buddhism & CJKV-E Dictionary (Charles Muller / University of Tokyo)](http://www.buddhism-dict.net/) - An authoritative, peer-reviewed lexicon of East Asian Buddhist technical terms, persons, and texts, accessible via free guest login.
- [ZDic (Handian)](https://www.zdic.net/) - A comprehensive digital Chinese dictionary integrating classical definitions from the Shuowen Jiezi, Kangxi Zidian, and modern character etymologies.
- [Chinese Etymology (Richard Sears)](https://hanziyuan.net/) - A searchable paleographical archive displaying the historical evolution of individual characters from oracle bone and bronze script to seal script and modern standard forms.

## Biographical, Historical, and Prosopographical Databases

Databases that document historical context, bureaucratic careers, kinship structures, and intellectual lineages of Chinese thinkers.

- [China Biographical Database Project (Harvard University, Academia Sinica & Peking University)](https://projects.iq.harvard.edu/cbdb) - A freely accessible relational database tracking biographical data, teacher-student relationships, and social networks for over 500,000 historical Chinese figures.
- [China Historical GIS (Harvard University & Fudan University)](https://chgis.fas.harvard.edu/) - A digital historical atlas providing spatial and temporal datasets to locate historical settlements, administrative units, and regional academies across imperial dynasties.

## Academic Societies, Scholarly Portals, and Bibliographic Registries

Scholarly clearinghouses, academic associations, and subject-specific bibliographies that track research developments, conferences, and literature in Chinese and comparative philosophy.

- [Warp, Weft, and Way (Stephen Angle & Manyul Im, eds.)](https://warpweftandway.com/) - An active group blog and academic clearinghouse devoted to Chinese and comparative philosophy, hosting substantive debates, book announcements, and podcast episodes.
- [PhilPapers: Chinese Philosophy (JeeLoo Liu, ed.)](https://philpapers.org/browse/chinese-philosophy) - A comprehensive bibliographic database indexing tens of thousands of contemporary academic books, journal articles, and preprints across all subfields of Chinese thought.
- [International Society for Chinese Philosophy (ISCP)](https://iscp-online1.org/) - The premier international academic society dedicated to Chinese philosophy, hosting biennial conferences and supporting cross-cultural philosophical exchange.
- [Society for Asian and Comparative Philosophy (SACP)](https://www.sacpweb.org/) - A scholarly organization promoting comparative work between Asian and Western traditions, hosting annual panels and monograph series.
- [The Golden Elixir: Taoism and Taoist Alchemy (Fabrizio Pregadio)](https://www.goldenelixir.com/) - A specialized academic reference portal providing bibliographies, glossaries, textual introductions, and canonical indices for Daoist philosophy and internal alchemy (*neidan*).
